stashy

Native Stash client for iPhone. Fast, no tracking, wired to your server.

App Store Discord GitHub

stashy is a native iOS and iPadOS client for your self-hosted Stash media server. It connects directly to your GraphQL endpoint and gives you a fast, polished interface for browsing, playing, and managing your library. Without any third-party tracking.

Core features

  • Direct connection: Connect to your Stash server via GraphQL (Stash v0.26+).
  • Library browsing: Scenes, Performers, Studios, Galleries, Tags, Groups, and Markers.
  • Search & filters: Advanced filtering and sorting across all content types.
  • Video playback: Built-in player with HLS streaming, quality selection, subtitles, PiP, and resume support.
  • Feeds: Scroll through scenes, markers, clips, and images in a reels-style view.
  • Customizable home: Configure dashboard rows, tabs, and layout to match how you browse.
  • Multi-server: Save and switch between multiple Stash servers.
  • Device sync: Support for Handy, Intiface, and LoveSpouse during playback.
  • Privacy first: Direct communication with your server only. No analytics or third-party tracking.

Introducing stashy+

stashy+ unlocks premium features with an optional subscription or one-time Lifetime purchase. Core browsing and playback stay free.

Included with stashy+:

  • AI Subs: On-device live captions and translation in the scene player (beta)
  • AI Motion: Real-time video analysis to sync connected devices to on-screen motion (beta)
  • Downloads: Save scenes for offline viewing
  • Tools: Overview statistics, O-Count heatmap, Session Timeline, Charts, Performer Match, and RateMe
  • Custom app icons: Personalize stashy on your Home Screen

Available as Monthly, Yearly, or Lifetime. Users who purchased stashy before 3.0 receive stashy+ Lifetime via Restore Purchases.

Requirements: Self-hosted Stash server (v0.26+).
