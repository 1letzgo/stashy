tasmoty

Scans your local network, tells you what is on it, and switches what can be switched.

App Store

tasmoty sweeps your Wi-Fi network, identifies every host it finds and shows you a clean list — with status, and a switch where the device supports one. Everything happens on the device, straight to your LAN. No cloud account, no bridge, no analytics.

What it finds

  • Switches and plugs: Tasmota and Shelly, with power relays you can toggle right from the list.
  • Lights: Yeelight, WLED, Philips Hue.
  • Infrastructure: NAS boxes, self-hosted services, cameras, printers, wallboxes.
  • Everything else: unknown hosts are still listed with what could be identified about them.

How it works

  • Parallel scan: probes the whole subnet concurrently, so a sweep takes seconds instead of minutes.
  • Local only: talks to your LAN directly — self-signed certificates are accepted for local addresses only.
  • Saved devices: found devices stick around between launches, no re-scan needed to flip a switch.

Requirements: iPhone on the same Wi-Fi network as the devices you want to see.
